# Pew Research Center - Climate Opinion Data 2024

**Source:** https://www.pewresearch.org/science/2024/12/09/how-americans-view-climate-change-and-policies-to-address-the-issue/
**Survey Date:** October 21-27, 2024
**Sample Size:** 9,593 U.S. adults

## Key Metrics for Socioeconomic Indicators

### Belief in Human-Caused Climate Change
- **45%** say human activity contributes **a great deal** to climate change
- **29%** say it contributes **some**
- **25%** say humans contribute **not too much or not at all**
- **Total believing humans contribute (great deal + some): 74%**

### Willingness to Make Sacrifices
- **75%** expect to make either major or minor sacrifices due to climate change
- **23%** expect to make **major sacrifices**
- **52%** expect to make **minor sacrifices**
- **25%** expect to make **no sacrifices**

### Policy Support (High Support Policies)
- **89%** favor planting trillion trees to absorb carbon
- **84%** favor requiring oil/gas companies to seal methane leaks
- **83%** favor tax credit for home energy efficiency improvements
- **79%** favor tax credit for carbon capture technology

### Perception of Economic Impact
- **34%** say climate policies **help the economy**
- **34%** say climate policies **hurt the economy**
- **32%** neutral/don't know

### Assessment of Action by Various Actors
- **69%** say large businesses doing **too little** on climate
- **60%** say state officials doing **too little**
- **57%** say energy industry doing **too little**
- **57%** say ordinary Americans doing **too little**

### Partisan Divide (Critical for Socioeconomic Analysis)
**Democrats:**
- 70% say humans contribute a great deal to climate change
- 52% say climate policies help the economy
- 75% say climate policies do more environmental good than harm

**Republicans:**
- 20% say humans contribute a great deal to climate change
- 56% say climate policies hurt the economy
- 28% say climate policies do more environmental good than harm

## Proposed Socioeconomic Indicators

### 1. Social Acceptance Score (0-100)
- **Formula:** (% believing humans contribute greatly + 0.5 × % believing humans contribute some)
- **2024 Value:** 45 + (0.5 × 29) = **59.5%**

### 2. Sacrifice Willingness Index (0-100)
- **Formula:** (% expecting major sacrifices × 1.0 + % expecting minor sacrifices × 0.5)
- **2024 Value:** (23 × 1.0) + (52 × 0.5) = **49%**

### 3. Policy Support Index (0-100)
- **Formula:** Average of high-support policies (tree planting, methane sealing, efficiency credits, carbon capture)
- **2024 Value:** (89 + 84 + 83 + 79) / 4 = **83.75%**

### 4. Political Polarization Factor
- **Formula:** Absolute difference between Democrat and Republican "great deal" belief
- **2024 Value:** |70 - 20| = **50 percentage points** (high polarization = slower action)

**Key Trend Periods:**

2. **2007-2011**: Sharp decline (41% → 25%) - Climategate, recession
3. **2011-2015**: Slow recovery (25% → 32%)
4. **2016-2017**: Rapid surge (32% → 45%) - Warm winters, Trump concerns
5. **2017-2024**: High plateau (42-45%) - Sustained elevated concern

**Partisan Divide Growth:**
- 2001: Democrats 46%, Republicans 47% (1-point gap)
- 2016: Democrats 66%, Republicans 29% (37-point gap)
- 2021: Democrats 82%, Republicans 29% (53-point gap)
